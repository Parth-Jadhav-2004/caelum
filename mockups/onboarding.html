How Caelum handles your data

Caelum sends audio to your configured transcription provider (Groq by default) over HTTPS. Optional cleanup text is sent to its configured provider. Recordings are never saved locally. Keys use Secret Service when available, otherwise an owner-only file with explicit disclosure.
